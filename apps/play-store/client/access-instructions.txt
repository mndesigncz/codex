Sign in with the demo guest account from the App access fields. The fictional venue "Ukázková kavárna" is not in the public list: on the first tab (Podniky) tap "Zadat kód podniku" and enter ukazkova-kavarna. Try Rezervace (booking), Objednat (table order, location check is off for the demo venue) and Karta (QR loyalty card). The interface is currently Czech only. No purchases in the app. Account deletion: Profil > Smazat účet.
