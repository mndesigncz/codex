Sign in with the demo guest account from the App access fields. The fictional venue "Café Demo" appears on the first tab (Podniky); if not visible, tap "Zadat kód podniku" and enter cafe-demo. Try Rezervace (booking), Objednat (table order, location check is off for the demo venue) and Karta (QR loyalty card). The interface is currently Czech only. No purchases in the app. Account deletion: Profil > Smazat účet.
