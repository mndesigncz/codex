Sign in with the demo manager account from the App access fields (fictional venue "Café Demo" with sample shifts, stock, tasks and chat). Main sections: Přehled (dashboard), Rozvrh (shift rota), Sklad (stock), Úkoly (tasks), Chat, Uzávěrky (closings). The interface is currently Czech only. The app has no purchases, prices or links to buy. Account deletion: Settings > Account > Smazat účet.
