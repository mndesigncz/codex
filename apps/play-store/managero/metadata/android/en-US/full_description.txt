Managero is an operations app for cafés, bars and restaurants. Managers and staff keep everything that matters in one place: on a phone, on a tablet behind the bar or on a computer.

WHAT IT DOES
• Shifts and rota: build the schedule, collect team availability, swap shifts, request time off and see "my shifts".
• Attendance: clock-ins and clock-outs, worked-hours overview.
• Tasks and procedures: daily tasks, opening and closing checklists, step-by-step guides.
• Stock: inventory levels, goods in, stocktake, shopping list and supplier orders.
• Recipes and menu: cost per portion, the venue's menu.
• Closing: end-of-shift closing with a check of required items.
• Chat and announcements: team chat, direct messages, announcements and polls.
• Rewards and ideas: points for extra work and an idea board for improving daily operations.
• Kiosk: a shared tablet behind the bar where staff sign in by tapping their name.
• Notifications about shifts, messages and low stock. Every kind can be switched off.

WHO IT IS FOR
Owners and managers who want their venue under control, and baristas and cooks who want to know when they work and what to do.

NOTES
• The app interface is currently available in Czech only.
• You need a venue account or an invitation from management to sign in. You can delete your account inside the app.
• Guests of venues use the separate app Managero client.
