WHAT THIS APP IS
Managero is a B2B operations app for cafes and restaurants (schedule, time tracking, stock, end-of-day closing, tasks, team chat) for owners, managers and employees. Guests use a SEPARATE app, "Managero client", not part of this submission.
The interface follows the device language (Czech original; English, German, Slovak, Polish machine-translated). Labels below: Czech (English).

HOW TO SIGN IN
The Demo Account fields contain the owner of a fictional venue "Café Demo" (manager role, sees everything). A second account has the employee role:
  e-mail:   {{REVIEW_MANAGERO_EMPLOYEE_USER}}
  password: {{REVIEW_MANAGERO_EMPLOYEE_PASSWORD}}
No 2FA. All Café Demo data is fictional, prepared for the review. Guest accounts do not work here; the app says so.

NO SIGN-UP AND NO PURCHASES IN THE APP (guidelines 3.1.1 and 3.1.3(f))
Managero is a free companion app to our paid web service for businesses. No purchases, prices, plans, links or calls to action to buy.
A business cannot be created in the app: "Vytvořit účet" (Create account) only explains that business accounts are created on the web, with no link. People join an existing business with a team code or invitation. Employees never pay.

WHERE TO FIND THINGS (manager account)
- Navigation (bottom bar + "Více" (More) on iPhone, side bar on iPad): "Přehled" (Overview), "Rozvrh" (Schedule; "Vygenerovat rozvrh" (Generate schedule)), "Sklad" (Stock), "Úkoly" (Tasks), "Chat", "Uzávěrky" (Closings).
- Account menu (avatar): "Tým" (Team), "Nastavení" (Settings), "Managero client" (managing the guest side: reservations, orders, loyalty).
- Shared bar iPad: "Tým" (Team) > "Tablet" sets a tablet sign-in; signed in with it, the iPad shows the kiosk where staff tap their name to clock in.
- Employee account: "Přehled" (Overview), "Moje směny" (My shifts), "Úkoly" (Tasks), "Postupy" (Procedures), "Chat".

NATIVE FEATURES (guideline 4.2)
APNs push (new shift, schedule change, low stock, chat; categories in Settings), native QR scanner for guest cards and coupons at the till, camera and photos for receipts and stock, share sheet with real files for exports (CSV, printable documents), universal links (invitations), iPad kiosk layout, external links in an in-app Safari view.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (guideline 1.2)
Chat and ideas exist only inside one business. Members can report content and block people; the owner removes members and content ("Nastavení" > "Nahlášený obsah", Settings > Reported content). Every report also reaches us, the platform operator, and is handled within 24 hours. No public content.

ACCOUNT DELETION (guideline 5.1.1(v))
"Nastavení" > "Data a soukromí" > "Smazat účet" (Settings > Data and privacy > Delete account), inside the app. An owner with other members must hand over or delete the business first; the app explains it.

PERMISSIONS
Camera and photos (receipts, stock, guest cards), location only when setting the venue position, notifications after an in-app explanation. All requested at the moment of use.

If anything does not work, please reply in App Store Connect; we answer within one working day.
