Managero is an operations app for cafes, restaurants, bistros and tea rooms. Shifts, time tracking, stock, recipes, tasks and end-of-day closings live in one place, on every team member's phone.

FOR MANAGERS
• Rota: a generator proposes shifts based on availability, you review and publish them with one tap.
• Time and earnings: who is on shift right now, hours worked, time-off and shift-swap requests.
• Closing: the till counted note by note, and a difference that cannot be hidden. The closing stays locked until required tasks are done.
• Stock and recipes: what is running low, what to reorder, margins calculated from ingredients. The app emails the supplier order for you.
• Tasks and procedures: opening and closing checklists, step-by-step guides right on the shift.
• Team, roles and permissions: whoever should not see wages does not see them. Custom roles with a choice of features.
• Multiple venues: each location has its own rota, stock and closings, and management sees them all together.
• Guest management: reservations, table orders, loyalty, coupons, events and news for guests using the Managero client app.

FOR STAFF
• My shifts, availability and time-off requests.
• Ticking off tasks and procedures during the shift.
• Team chat, announcements and polls.
• Rewards and ideas to improve operations.

TABLET BEHIND THE BAR
Kiosk mode for a shared iPad: tap your name instead of a password, clock in and out, shift tasks and closing. A shared device has no password to leak.

NOTIFICATIONS
New shift, rota change, low stock, a chat message. Every notification category can be turned off in settings.

ACCOUNT
You need a business account to sign in. Management creates the business and invites the team with a code or by email. The app is for management and staff of businesses, not for guests. Guests have their own app, Managero client.

Your data belongs to your business. You can delete your account in the app.

The app interface is currently available in Czech only.
