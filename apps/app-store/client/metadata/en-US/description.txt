Managero client is an app for guests of cafes, restaurants, bistros and tea rooms that use Managero. Your loyalty card, reservations and table orders in one place.

CARD
• One QR code for every venue. Show it at the till and the staff adds a stamp, points or credit.
• Your card also shows without a connection, handy where the signal is poor.
• In every venue you see your points, stamps, credit and coupons.

FIND A VENUE
• Scan the QR code on a table or at the counter, or search by name or street.
• Menu, opening hours, events and news from the venue.

RESERVATIONS
• Pick a day, a time and the number of guests, the confirmation arrives as a notification.
• Add a confirmed reservation to your calendar.

ORDER FROM YOUR TABLE
• Scan the table QR code, choose from the menu and send the order to the staff. You pay the staff, nothing is paid in the app.
• Follow the order status right in the app.
• A venue may ask you to confirm you are on site. Your location is used only at the moment you order.

LOYALTY
• Stamp campaigns, points and membership levels.
• Coupons and promo codes from venues.
• Invite a friend and you both get points (where the venue offers it).

NOTIFICATIONS
Reservation confirmations and order status arrive as notifications. News and events from venues are sent only if you explicitly allow it, and you can turn them off at any time.

PRIVACY
You can delete your account at any time inside the app, in the Profile section.

The app works with venues that use Managero. Cannot find your venue? Tell them about us.

The app interface is currently available in Czech only.
