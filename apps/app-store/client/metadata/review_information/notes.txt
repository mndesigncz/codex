WHAT THIS APP IS
Managero client is the guest-facing app for cafes, restaurants and bistros that run on Managero (our business app, submitted separately as "Managero", app.managero.app). A guest gets one loyalty card (QR code) for all venues, can reserve a table and order from the table. The interface is currently Czech only.

HOW TO SIGN IN
Use the demo account in the Demo Account fields (guest role, pre-verified, no 2FA). You can also register your own account with any e-mail address; e-mail verification is not required to use the app.
Do not use a manager account of the other app here: this app will tell you that such an account belongs to the business app.

DEMO VENUE
"Ukázková kavárna" is a fictional demo venue. It is not listed in the public directory. On the first tab ("Podniky") tap "Zadat kód podniku" and enter:  ukazkova-kavarna
The demo account is already a member of the venue with points, stamps and one coupon.

STEP BY STEP
1. Karta tab: your QR code and 8-digit code, points, stamps, coupons per venue. The card also opens in airplane mode (cached).
2. Open "Ukázková kavárna" > Rezervace: choose a day, time and number of guests, send. The status becomes "Čeká na potvrzení". A manager confirms it in the business app and the guest gets a push notification (needs a real device).
3. Open the venue > Objednat: the demo venue has the location and QR checks switched off, so you can order from anywhere. Add items and send. Payment is made with the staff after the meal; nothing is paid in the app.
4. Věrnost: stamp campaigns and coupons. Stamps and points are added by staff at the till when they scan the guest QR code; guests cannot add them to themselves.
5. Profil: notification switches, Zásady soukromí, Podmínky, Podpora, Odhlásit se and "Smazat účet" (account deletion inside the app).

NATIVE FEATURES (guideline 4.2)
QR scanner with the camera (venue and table codes), push notifications through APNs (reservation and order status), offline card, universal links (venue QR codes open the app), native share sheet and calendar export of a confirmed reservation.

PAYMENTS
No purchases, no payment cards, no top-ups in the app. Orders are paid at the venue (goods and services consumed outside the app, guideline 3.1.3(e)).

USER-GENERATED CONTENT
Guests write only private notes (reservation note, order note, rating text) that are visible to the venue staff only. There is no public feed and no comments between guests.

PUSH AND MARKETING (guideline 4.5.4)
Transactional notifications (reservation, order) only. Marketing messages from venues are sent only after an explicit opt-in switch ("Novinky a akce") that is off by default.

PERMISSIONS
Camera (QR scanning), location while using the app (only when ordering from a table, to confirm the guest is on site; coordinates are not stored), notifications. All are requested at the moment of use.
