WHAT THIS APP IS
Managero client is the guest app for cafes and restaurants that run on Managero (our business app, submitted separately as "Managero"). One loyalty card (QR code) for all venues, table reservations, ordering from the table, stamps, points and coupons.
The interface follows the device language (Czech original; English, German, Slovak, Polish machine-translated). Labels below: Czech (English).

HOW TO SIGN IN
Use the demo account in the Demo Account fields (guest, no 2FA), or register with any e-mail (no verification). Business accounts do not work here; the app says so.

DEMO VENUE
"Café Demo" (fictional) is listed on the first tab "Podniky" (Places). Or tap "Naskenovat" (Scan) in the middle of the bottom bar: the native QR scanner opens; close it and type cafe-demo into "Kód podniku" (Place code) > "Otevřít podnik" (Open place).

STEP BY STEP
1. "Moje" (My page): loyalty card (QR and 8-character code), points and stamps, one coupon ready to use, one confirmed reservation.
2. Offline card: open "Moje" once, switch on airplane mode, force-quit and reopen: the offline screen shows the card saved in the app storage, ready to scan.
3. Café Demo > "Rezervace" (Reservations): day, time, guests, send. Status "Čeká na potvrzení" (Waiting for confirmation); when the venue confirms it, a push notification arrives.
4. Café Demo > "Objednat" (Order): location and table-QR checks are off for the demo venue. Pick items, send; paid to the staff at the venue, nothing is paid in the app. Status updates arrive as notifications.
5. Event "Degustace kávy" > "Přidat do kalendáře" (Add to calendar) and "Moje" > "Uložit kartu jako obrázek" (Save card as image) open the share sheet with the file.
6. Avatar (top right) > "Účet a soukromí" (Account and privacy): notifications, opt-in for news from venues (off by default), privacy policy, terms, support and "Smazat účet" (Delete account) inside the app.

NATIVE FEATURES (guideline 4.2)
Native QR scanner (ML Kit) for venue and table codes, APNs push (reservation, order status), loyalty card in native storage shown offline, universal links (printed QR codes open the app), share sheet with real files, haptics, external links (maps) in an in-app Safari view.

PAYMENTS
No purchases, cards or top-ups in the app. Orders are paid at the venue (goods and services outside the app, 3.1.3(e)).

USER-GENERATED CONTENT
Guests write only private notes (reservation, order, rating) visible to the venue staff. No public feed, no contact between guests.

PUSH AND MARKETING (guideline 4.5.4)
Transactional notifications only. Marketing from venues only after the opt-in switch "Novinky a akce od podniků" (News and offers from businesses), off by default.

PERMISSIONS
Camera only when you tap Scan. Location only when ordering at a venue that requires it (not the demo venue); only the distance is stored. Notifications after an in-app explanation. All requested at the moment of use.

CONTACT
If anything does not work, please reply in App Store Connect; we answer within one working day.
